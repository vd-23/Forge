# M2 — Insight

Turn the logged data into something worth looking at, and close the two gaps M1
left behind.

## Shape

Five tabs: **Home · Workout · Exercises · History · Settings**.

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| Home | Heatmap, streaks, this week, 30-day volume, recent PRs |
| Workout | Routines (unchanged), plus archived routines |
| Exercises | The library, moved out of Settings; tap an exercise → its progress charts |
| History | Session list (unchanged), plus delete and set editing |
| Settings | Units, rest, about |

## Deviations from the PRD

1. **No Progress tab.** Per-exercise charts live in Exercises → exercise detail.
   The exercise is the natural owner of its own trend line, and it keeps the
   dock at five.
2. **No Dashboard tab.** §7.6's content is the Home tab.
3. **Manage exercises leaves Settings** for its own tab (PRD §7.7).
4. **Heatmap intensity is working-set count**, closing the §10 open question.
   Volume would render every bodyweight day as the lightest shade.

## Checkpoint A — Gaps and tab restructure

**A1 · Delete a workout from history.** Swipe action on a history row →
confirmation → `context.delete(session)`, cascading to its exercises and sets.
Nothing else references a session, so there is no archive variant.

**A2 · Unarchive.** Both lists hide archived rows with no way back.
- `Features/Shared/ArchiveFilter.swift` — `enum ArchiveScope { case active, archived }`.
- Routine list and exercise library each get a toolbar toggle for showing
  archived rows, and an "Unarchive" swipe action on them.
- Archived rows are visually dimmed and cannot be started.

**A3 · Five tabs.** `RootView` gains Home and Exercises; `SettingsView` drops its
"Manage exercises" link. `ExerciseLibraryView` grows its own `NavigationStack`.

**Tests:** `ArchiveFilterTests`, plus a `SessionDeletionTests` covering the
cascade.

## Checkpoint B — ForgeCore consistency and series maths

Pure functions, TDD, no SwiftData. Sessions in, values out.

- `currentStreakDays(_:today:)` / `longestStreakDays(_:)` — PRD §6 semantics: a
  day counts if it holds a finished session; the current streak anchors to
  yesterday so it doesn't read 0 before the morning session.
- `heatmap(_:window:)` → `[Date: HeatLevel]`, levels from working-set count.
- `dailyVolumeSeries(_:window:)` and `weeklyVolumeSeries(_:weeks:)`.
- `e1rmSeries(_:exerciseID:)`, `volumeSeries(_:exerciseID:)`,
  `maxRepsSeries(_:exerciseID:)`, `addedWeightSeries(_:exerciseID:)`.
- `recentPRs(_:limit:)` — PR hits across history, newest first.

## Checkpoint C — Home

Heatmap (~18 weeks, horizontally scrollable, tap a day → that session), current
and longest streak, this week's workout count and volume, a 30-day volume chart,
and recent PRs. Swift Charts; solid backgrounds, glass only on chrome.

## Checkpoint D — Exercise detail and charts

Tap an exercise → name, flags, body part, lifetime PRs, and its trend chart with
a range control (8w / 6m / 1y / all). Weighted exercises get e1RM with a volume
toggle; bodyweight exercises get max reps and added weight. Edit and
archive/unarchive move here from the row's swipe actions.

## Checkpoint E — Edit past sets from History

Session detail becomes editable: change a set's load, reps or RPE, delete a set,
add one. Reuses `SetEntryRow`. Editing recomputes nothing — volume and PRs are
derived on read.

## Checkpoint F — `secondaryBodyParts`

`Exercise.secondaryBodyPartsRaw: [String]`, multi-select in the editor, and the
heatmap/Home body-part breakdown counts them at half weight.
